Security Incident Response Procedure. Severity 1 incidents (core banking down, confirmed data breach) must be acknowledged by the IT security team within 15 minutes and the Head of IT must be informed immediately. Severity 2 incidents (single branch network outage, malware on one computer) must be acknowledged within 1 hour. Phishing: Employees who receive a suspicious email must not click links or open attachments. Report it using the Report Phishing button or by calling the IT service desk on extension 4455. Data breach: Any suspected breach of customer data must be reported to the Compliance Officer within 2 hours of discovery. Records: Incident records are kept for 5 years. After each Severity 1 incident a review meeting is held within 5 working days.
